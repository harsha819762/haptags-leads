Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Haptags LLP ("Haptags", "we", "us", "our") operates haptags.com and provides Website Development, Mobile App Development, Digital Marketing, Interior Design, and Real Estate Advisory services from our office in Bangalore, Karnataka, India. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you:

  • Submit the consultation request form (name/organization, email, phone, service of interest, project details).
  • Request a brochure download (name, work email, contact number, service of interest).
  • Subscribe to our newsletter (email address).
  • Respond to a limited-time offer form, where applicable (name, email, phone, organization, address, and, where the offer requires identity verification for prize/coupon fulfilment, a government ID number and document upload).
  • Email, call, or message us directly.

We also automatically collect limited technical information standard to any website visit (such as pages viewed and general device/browser information via server logs) and your theme preference (light/dark), which is stored only in your browser's local storage and never transmitted to us.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to consultation requests and enquiries.
  • To deliver the brochures or offer benefits you requested.
  • To send newsletter updates, if you subscribed (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To verify eligibility and prevent fraud for limited-time promotional offers.
  • To improve our services and this website.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Government ID information (promotional offers only)

Where a specific limited-time offer requires identity verification to prevent abuse (for example, limiting one offer per household), any government ID number or document you submit is used solely for that verification, is not published, and is retained only as long as necessary to administer that specific offer before secure deletion. You may request early deletion by emailing us at the address below.

4. How we share information

We share information only with service providers who help us operate this website and respond to your enquiries — for example, our email delivery provider (Hostinger, for transactional email dispatch). These providers are only permitted to use your information to provide services to us. We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Cookies & local storage

This site does not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. We use your browser's local storage only to remember your light/dark theme preference. Google Fonts is loaded from Google's servers to display site typography, which may involve a standard, non-personalized network request to Google's font CDN.

6. Data retention

We retain enquiry and consultation data for as long as needed to respond to you and maintain business records, and no longer than necessary for that purpose or as required by law.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have submitted to us by emailing info@haptags.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information you share with us, including transmitting this website exclusively over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

Our services are directed at businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of this website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
